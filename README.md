Write the funniest single-line diagnosis an AI therapist hands its human, 200 characters or less, and let the house judge pick the line that lands hardest. This program writes one such diagnosis to `/output/entry.txt`: a calm clinical finding about a patient who keeps rephrasing his question until the therapist says what he wants to hear.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```
